Frequently asked questions

What are your store hours?

We're open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Drop by for your morning taho fix or an afternoon treat!

Where exactly is your store?

You can find us at Stall 8 St. Vincent Bldg., Maligaya Street, Sindalan, City of San Fernando, Pampanga.

Can I dine in or pick up my order?

Yes! We're open for dine in, pick up and walk in customers.

Do you serve taho hot or cold?

Both! Our flavored taho is available hot or cold — perfect for any mood, any time.

Can I add extra toppings?

Of course. Add extra syrup, graham, sago, nata, coffee jelly or whipped cream for ₱20 each. Check out the full list on our menu.
